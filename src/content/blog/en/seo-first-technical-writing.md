Technical blogs often sacrifice discoverability for depth. The good news is that great technical writing and strong SEO are not at odds — they reinforce each other.

## Write for humans first

Search engines reward content that satisfies user intent. If your article solves a real problem, uses clear headings, and provides actionable examples, readers will stay longer, share more, and signal quality to crawlers.

## Structure matters

Use a single H1 for the title, H2s for major sections, and H3s for subsections. This does three things:

1. **Screen readers** can navigate the page easily.
2. **Search engines** understand your content hierarchy.
3. **Readers** can scan and decide if the article is worth their time.

## Metadata you should not ignore

- **Title tag**: front-load keywords, stay under 60 characters.
- **Meta description**: a concise summary under 160 characters.
- **Canonical URL**: prevents duplicate content issues.
- **Open Graph image**: controls how your post looks when shared.

## Structured data

Adding JSON-LD `BlogPosting` markup helps search engines display rich snippets like author, publish date, and reading time directly in results.

## Final thought

SEO is not about stuffing keywords. It is about making your best content discoverable. Write well, structure clearly, and let the metadata do the rest.
